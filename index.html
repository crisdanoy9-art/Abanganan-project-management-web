PROJECT OVERVIEW

What is Abanganan?

Abanganan is a web-based platform that connects room seekers — students, workers, or anyone needing a place to stay — with landlords renting boarding houses and rooms in Dapitan City. Right now, finding a room means walking around and asking around, or posting publicly on Facebook groups where everyone can see it, and landlords have no central place to list their rooms.

Abanganan fixes that with three user types: Room Seekers, who browse, filter and privately inquire about listings; Landlords, who upload photos, prices, descriptions and pin their exact location on a map; and an Admin, who monitors the platform and tracks which areas of the city are most in demand.

Why it matters

  • Lets shy or introverted seekers look for a room privately, with no public posting
  • Gives seekers one place to browse every available room in Dapitan City
  • Lets seekers filter by barangay, price, and distance to school or work
  • Gives landlords an easy way to list a property with exact map location
  • Enables private, direct inquiries with no public exposure for either side
  • Gives the admin visibility into rental trends across the city

PROJECT SCOPE

What is included in the system

✓ In Scope

  • Landlord accounts: create, edit or take down listings anytime
  • Listing uploads with photos, price, description and amenities
  • Exact property location pinned on a map
  • Room seeker browsing of all listings with full details
  • Filtering by barangay or price range
  • Search for the nearest available room to a reference point (school/work)
  • Private inquiry between a seeker and a landlord
  • Admin monitoring, listing removal and landlord verification
  • Admin dashboard with basic rental trends per barangay
  • Geographic coverage limited to Dapitan City

✕ Out of Scope

  • Online payment, deposit or booking/reservation processing
  • Digital lease or rental contract generation and e-signatures
  • Tenant background or credit checks
  • Listings for areas outside Dapitan City
  • Integration with government housing/permit systems
  • Real-time chat — inquiries are private messages, not live chat

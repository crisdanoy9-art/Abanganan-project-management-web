2 Communication Objectives

01

To keep all team members informed about project progress, deadlines and task assignments.

02

To give the project adviser clear and timely progress reports and deliverables for review and approval.

03

To collect feedback from end users and turn it into improvements to the system.

04

To coordinate the designers, developers and testers so blockers are raised and solved quickly.

05

To record decisions, documents and test results in one shared place that everyone can find.
